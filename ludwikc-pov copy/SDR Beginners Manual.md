2. What are the activities & metrics that are expected from an SDR?
   As an SDR, you will be expected to schedule a certain amount of qualified meetings a month or a quarter. On top of this, you'll have to make anywhere from 20-100 interactions (cold calls, cold emails, or LinkedIn messages) per day.
