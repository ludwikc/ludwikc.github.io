

##  **1. From Lifehakerzy.pl to Hackerzy.pl … and Back Again**

• **Lifehakerzy.pl** was originally a Polish-speaking productivity and self-development community.

• Ludwik, its co-founder and CEO at the time, left the previous company; the project was halted there.

• **Hackerzy.pl** then emerged under **Ludwik’s** sole direction, carrying forward the same “life-hacking” ethos—focusing on **mindset, personal growth, social skills, and everyday productivity hacks**.

• Over time, **some potential members assumed** it was a tech- or cybersecurity-centric group, due to the “Hackerzy” name, causing confusion.

  

**The Brand Renewal**

• **Lifehakerzy.pl** (and Lifehackerzy.pl) domains have become available again.

• Rebranding **Hackerzy.pl → Lifehackerzy.pl** aims to clarify that “hacking” refers to **self-improvement and life optimization**, not cybersecurity or illicit hacking.

  

## **2. Hackerzy.pl: A Life-Hacking Community**

  

**Core Focus**

• **Life Hacking & Mindset Building**: Emphasizes productivity, mental resilience, communication skills, and personal growth strategies.

• **Social & Emotional Development**: Encourages authentic relationships, community support, and peer-driven inspiration.

  

**Distinguishing Factors**

• **Not** about cybersecurity or “hackers-for-hire.”

_(No penetration testing or password-cracking services. That’s a completely different site!)_

• **Community-First Ethos**: Nurtured on Discourse, with day-to-day interactions on Discord.

• **Broad Skill-Building**: Spans from mindfulness to relationship insights, bridging all areas of personal growth.

  

## **3. Why Rename to Lifehackerzy.pl**

1. **Clarity**: “Lifehackerzy” instantly suggests hacking life’s obstacles rather than computers.

2. **Continuation**: Many original members from Lifehakerzy.pl still follow Ludwik; reusing the name ties everything back to their roots.

3. **Inclusivity**: People unfamiliar with tech or coding will recognize it’s about **improving daily life**—not code exploits.
