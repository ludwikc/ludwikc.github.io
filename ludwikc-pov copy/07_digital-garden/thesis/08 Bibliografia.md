
### Academic Papers

1. **Rusbult, C. E., & Van Lange, P. A. M. (2003)**. "Interdependence, Interaction, and Relationships." *Annual Review of Psychology*, 54(1), 351-375.
   - Discusses the dynamics of interdependence in relationships, relevant for understanding the Michelangelo Effect.

2. **Agnew, C. R., Van Lange, P. A. M., Rusbult, C. E., & Langston, C. A. (1998)**. "Cognitive Interdependence: The Role of the Michelangelo Phenomenon in Relationship Development." *Journal of Personality and Social Psychology*, 74(4), 939-953.
   - This paper directly addresses the Michelangelo Effect and its implications in romantic relationships.

3. **Kenny, D. A., & Acitelli, L. K. (2001)**. "The Role of Idealization in Relationships." *Journal of Social and Personal Relationships*, 18(4), 507-525.
   - Explores how idealization plays a role in partner support and development.

4. **Murray, S. L., & Holmes, J. G. (1997)**. "The Benefits of Positive Illusions: Idealization and the Maintenance of Relationships." *Journal of Personality and Social Psychology*, 72(6), 1185-1199.
   - Discusses how positive illusions about partners can foster relationship maintenance.

5. **Baldwin, M. W., & Sinclair, L. (1996)**. "Self-esteem and the Michelangelo Phenomenon: The Role of Self-Verification in Relationship Development." *Journal of Personality and Social Psychology*, 71(3), 491-504.
   - Examines self-esteem's influence on the Michelangelo Effect within relationships.

### Books

1. **Murray, S., & Holmes, J. G. (2010)**. *The Handbook of Personal Relationships*. Wiley.
   - This comprehensive guide covers various aspects of personal relationships, including idealization and support mechanisms.

2. **Reis, H. T., & Shaver, P. (1988)**. *Intimacy as an Interpersonal Process*. In *Handbook of Personal Relationships* (pp. 367-389). Wiley.
   - Offers insights into intimacy processes that relate to the Michelangelo Effect.

3. **Hendrick, S., & Hendrick, C. (2000)**. *Romantic Relationships*. Sage Publications.
   - Discusses various theories related to romantic relationships, including support dynamics.

4. **Feeney, J. A., & Noller, P. (1990)**. *Adult Attachment: Theory, Research, and Clinical Implications*. Sage Publications.
   - Provides a foundational understanding of attachment theory relevant to relationship dynamics.

### Additional Documents

1. **Van Lange, P.A.M., et al.(2006)**: "The Impact of Partner Support on Personal Growth." *Personal Relationships*.
   - A review article that discusses how partner support can facilitate personal development.

2. **Duncan, L.E., et al.(2009)**: "The Role of Communication in Romantic Relationships." *Journal of Family Psychology*.
   - Analyzes how communication patterns affect relationship dynamics and personal growth.

3. **Clark, M.S., & Mills, J.(1993)**: "The Difference Between Communal and Exchange Relationships: What It Is and Is Not." *Personality and Social Psychology Bulletin*.
   - Explores different relationship types which may influence the Michelangelo Effect.
